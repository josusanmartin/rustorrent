# Rustorrent 0.2 Release Candidate 3

The third release candidate for 0.2 is about connectivity. Rustorrent now gets through a second router, reaches peers behind routers, and keeps uploading when nobody can connect in. It also fixes uTP transfers from qBittorrent and other libtorrent clients, and makes it easy to set up search.

- **Linux x86_64:** `rustorrent-0.2.0-rc.3-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` (needs OpenSSL 3)
- ZIP archives of the app and `SHA256SUMS.txt` are attached below. The app requires macOS 11 or newer.

The app is ad-hoc signed for integrity. It is **not Developer ID signed or Apple notarized**, so on first launch macOS may ask you to allow it: right-click the app, choose **Open**, or allow it in **System Settings › Privacy & Security**.

## Connectivity

- **A second router.** When your router's own internet address is private, another router or your provider's modem sits in front of it (double NAT). Rustorrent now also asks that outer device to open the port, over NAT-PMP or UPnP. If the outer device refuses, the connectivity card says what to change on it: bridge mode, a port forward to your router, or DMZ.
- **Carrier-grade NAT is named as such.** If your provider shares one public address between customers, the card says so, and says that no router setting can change it.
- **Hole punching (BEP 55).** Two peers that cannot accept incoming connections can now reach each other through a peer they are both connected to. Rustorrent can make the introduction, respond to one, and ask for one. qBittorrent, Deluge and other libtorrent clients support the same extension.
- **One port for the DHT and uTP.** The DHT now uses the forwarded port instead of a random one, so DHT nodes and peers learn a port that actually reaches you.
- **More outgoing connections when nobody can connect in.** After five minutes without an incoming peer, the Balanced and Aggressive profiles open more connections (Balanced: 300 in total, 60 per transfer). Limits you set yourself are kept.
- **Softer wording.** "Not reachable" is now **Outgoing only**. Downloads and uploads continue over the connections Rustorrent opens, and the card says so, next to the steps that would add incoming peers.

## Fixed

- **uTP transfers from libtorrent.** Rustorrent reset any uTP connection whose peer sent a data packet larger than 1,200 bytes. libtorrent, the engine in qBittorrent and Deluge, fills the network's packet size, so those downloads stopped at the first full packet. Packets up to 16 KB are now accepted.

## Search

- **Install recommended plugins.** On a fresh install, the search view offers to install the recommended plugins in one step: The Pirate Bay, LimeTorrents, EZTV, SolidTorrents, TorLock, TorrentProject and Torrents.csv. These are the official ones maintained by the qBittorrent project. The same button is in the plugin settings, and on the command line it is `rustorrent remote plugin recommended`.
- **No adult sites in the community catalog.** Plugins for adult sites are no longer listed.

## Validation

- **Rust:** 535 unit tests, 9 process tests and 21 transport tests.
- **Real processes:** 10 transfer, crash-recovery and hole-punching scenarios. The hole-punching tests use real sockets, with Rustorrent acting both as the introducer and as the peer that connects.
- **libtorrent interoperability (new):** downloads from and uploads to libtorrent over uTP only. This test failed before the uTP fix.
- **Transmission interoperability:** seeding on the local network, uploading while downloading, and encrypted transfers.
- **Browser flows** with WCAG A/AA accessibility checks.
- **CI** covers Linux, macOS and Windows, Rust 1.89, dependency advisories and the license notice.
