# Rustorrent 0.2 Release Candidate 4

The fourth release candidate for 0.2 answers "why am I not uploading?". The Peers tab now lists every connected peer, what it has, and what it is doing with you.

![Rustorrent 0.2 RC 4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/josusanmartin/rustorrent/v0.2.0-rc.4/docs/screenshots/hero-light.png)

## Download

- **Apple silicon:** `Rustorrent-0.2.0-rc.4-arm64.dmg`
- **Intel or Apple silicon:** `Rustorrent-0.2.0-rc.4-universal.dmg`
- **Linux x86_64:** `rustorrent-0.2.0-rc.4-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` (needs OpenSSL 3)
- ZIP archives of the app and `SHA256SUMS.txt` are attached below. The app requires macOS 11 or newer.

The app is ad-hoc signed for integrity. It is **not Developer ID signed or Apple notarized**, so on first launch macOS may ask you to allow it: right-click the app, choose **Open**, or allow it in **System Settings › Privacy & Security**.

## Connected peers

Open a transfer and choose **Peers**. Below the totals, a table lists each connected peer:

- **Peer:** its address, and whether the connection is TCP or uTP, incoming or outgoing, and encrypted.
- **Client:** the program it runs, read from its peer ID, such as qBittorrent 4.6.5 or Transmission 4.0.5.
- **Has:** how much of the torrent it has.
- **Down and Up:** the current speed from and to that peer.
- **State:** what it is doing with you:
  - **Downloading from you:** it wants data and you are sending it.
  - **Waiting for an upload slot:** it wants data, and gets a turn when a slot frees up.
  - **Has everything (seed):** it needs nothing, so there is nothing to upload to it.
  - **Wants nothing from you yet:** you have no piece it is missing.

Uploading only happens when a peer is missing a piece you have. On an older torrent with many seeds, every peer may show **Has everything**, and 0 B uploaded is then expected, whatever the client.

The list is also available to scripts at `GET /torrent/peers?id=<transfer id>`.

## Validation

- **Rust:** 538 unit tests, 9 process tests and 21 transport tests.
- **Real processes:** 10 transfer, crash-recovery and hole-punching scenarios.
- **libtorrent interoperability:** downloads and uploads over uTP. The upload test now reads the peer list mid-transfer and checks that it names libtorrent and shows it downloading from Rustorrent.
- **Transmission interoperability:** seeding on the local network, uploading while downloading, and encrypted transfers.
- **Browser flows** with WCAG A/AA accessibility checks.
- **CI** covers Linux, macOS and Windows, Rust 1.89, dependency advisories and the license notice.
