# Rustorrent 0.2 Release Candidate 9

Fixes a download stall when large piece buffers are occupied and connected peers have other pieces available ([#17](https://github.com/josusanmartin/rustorrent/issues/17#issuecomment-7210185168)).

- The shared buffer allowance grows with the number of connected peers and the torrent's piece size. It starts at about 64 MiB per torrent and can grow to about 1 GiB per torrent, with a process-wide cap of about 1.25 GiB. Buffers are allocated on demand.
- A wire regression test keeps four 16 MiB buffers occupied and verifies that a fifth peer can deliver a later piece in sequential mode.

Throughput on the reported Linux/NTFS swarm has yet to be measured with this build.
